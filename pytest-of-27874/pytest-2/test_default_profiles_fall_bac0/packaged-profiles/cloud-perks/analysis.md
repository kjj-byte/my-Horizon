# Evaluation goal

Evaluate the practical value of cloud resource perks, AI student discounts, redeem codes, cloud credits, Google Pro perks, free domains, and invite free trials for developers, students, and engineers looking to build projects at low or zero cost.

# Scoring rubric

- **9-10: Exceptional value.** Genuinely no-threshold, high-utility benefits: verified GPT / Gemini Advanced student free passes or large discounts, verified active 100% free domains (e.g. GitHub Student Pack .me, us.kg), permanent free VPS / compute tiers (Oracle Always Free), Google Cloud $300+ credits, universal zero-threshold redeem codes (兑换码) for Pro tools or large AI tokens without requiring credit card.
- **7-8: High value.** High practical utility: official student verification perks (GitHub Student Pack, JetBrains edu, Azure for Students $100), light-task cloud credits (Huawei Cloud/AWS labs), verified referral/invite bonuses that directly grant free tiers or tokens, Google Cloud/Pro certification perks, or verified substantial discount vouchers.
- **5-6: Moderate interest.** Standard limited-period free trials requiring credit card cancellation, moderate discount vouchers with minimum spending conditions, promo codes with strict limits, or referral systems requiring multiple invites.
- **3-4: Low priority.** High-threshold vouchers requiring expensive commitments, routine minor discount campaigns, or tasks where the time investment heavily outweighs the perk value.
- **0-2: Noise.** Expired promo codes or giveaways, misleading clickbait ("free" requiring non-refundable deposits), predatory referral scams, or purely commercial advertisements without developer benefits.

# Evaluation guidance

Prioritize authenticity, actual cost savings, accessibility (ease of claiming), and duration of the free resources. 

Explicitly identify and extract:
1. **福利类型 (Perk Type)**: 学生优惠 / 兑换码 / 云赠金 / 免费域名 / 邀请试用 / 认证福利
2. **领取门槛 (Prerequisites)**: 是否需要 edu 邮箱、是否需要海外信用卡、是否仅限新用户、是否需要完成特定任务
3. **兑换码/领取链接 (Code & Claim URL)**: 明确提取出的 Promo Code、Redeem Code 或官方直接领取的直达链接
4. **有效期与名额 (Validity)**: 明确活动结束日期或限额信息
