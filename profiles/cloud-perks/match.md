# Cloud perks and developer incentives profile

Use this profile for news, announcements, and community shares concerning cloud platform credits, free tiers, developer incentives, competitions, and developer discounts.

Typical items include:
- Major cloud providers' promotional and incentive campaigns, specifically Google Cloud, Huawei Cloud (华为云), AWS, Azure, Alibaba Cloud, Tencent Cloud, etc.
- Free cloud compute and resources: free ECS / VPS, trial quotas, Free Tier expansions, Flexus instance specials, and CodeArts developer tool credits.
- Financial vouchers and grants: 无门槛云资源券 (no-threshold vouchers), 代金券 (coupons/credits), 注册赠金 (registration bonus credits), and 学生优惠 (student discounts / education packs).
- Event-driven rewards: 开发者大赛 (developer competitions & hackathons with prize pools/compute subsidies) and 开发者任务奖励 (developer task & lab challenge rewards).

Do not use this profile for routine enterprise contract announcements without public developer access, general product updates with no incentives or perks, commercial pricing increases, paid enterprise case studies, or spammy affiliate schemes without verified value.
