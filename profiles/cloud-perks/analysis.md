# Evaluation goal

Evaluate the practical value of cloud resource perks, developer incentives, vouchers, free tiers, and competition subsidies for developers, students, and engineers looking to build projects at low or zero cost.

# Scoring rubric

- **9-10: Exceptional value.** Genuinely no-threshold substantial cloud vouchers (无门槛大额券), long-term or permanent free VPS/ECS instances, major developer competition prize pools with free compute grants, or large registration bonus credits (e.g. Google Cloud $300+, Huawei Cloud major hackathon prizes) with verified availability.
- **7-8: High value.** High-utility practical perks: light-task rewards (e.g. completing a CodeArts/Flexus lab to earn credits), verified student discount packages (学生优惠), substantial compute discounts, or verified free-tier resources for developers.
- **5-6: Moderate interest.** Standard limited-period trial quotas, moderate discount vouchers with minimum spending conditions, or developer competitions with smaller non-compute rewards.
- **3-4: Low priority.** High-threshold vouchers requiring expensive enterprise commitments, routine minor discount campaigns, or tasks where the time investment heavily outweighs the perk value.
- **0-2: Noise.** Expired promotions, misleading clickbait ("free" requiring non-refundable deposits), predatory referral scams, or purely commercial advertisements without developer benefits.

# Evaluation guidance

Prioritize authenticity, actual cost savings, accessibility (ease of claiming), and duration of the free resources. Explicitly note prerequisites such as real-name identity verification, student ID verification, or required task steps. Do not reward exaggerated clickbait headlines; focus on what developers can truly claim.

Use three to five specific topic tags (e.g. `#华为云`, `#Google Cloud`, `#代金券`, `#免费VPS`, `#开发者大赛`).
