# Evaluation goal

Evaluate the practical value of cloud resource perks, AI API token grants, developer incentives, vouchers, free tiers, and competition subsidies for developers, students, and engineers looking to build projects at low or zero cost.

# Scoring rubric

- **9-10: Exceptional value.** Genuinely no-threshold substantial cloud vouchers (无门槛大额券), long-term or permanent free VPS/ECS instances (e.g. Oracle Always Free), large AI token credits (e.g. Google Cloud $300, OpenAI/Claude/DeepSeek large free token allocations), major hackathons with free compute grants, or verified free domain/tool giveaways.
- **7-8: High value.** High-utility practical perks: light-task rewards (e.g. completing a Huawei Cloud/CodeArts lab for credits), verified student discount packages (GitHub Student Pack), free AI model inference tiers, substantial compute discounts, or verified free-tier resources with easy registration.
- **5-6: Moderate interest.** Standard limited-period trial quotas, moderate discount vouchers with minimum spending conditions, promo codes with restrictions, or developer competitions with smaller non-compute rewards.
- **3-4: Low priority.** High-threshold vouchers requiring expensive enterprise commitments, routine minor discount campaigns, or tasks where the time investment heavily outweighs the perk value.
- **0-2: Noise.** Expired promotions, misleading clickbait ("free" requiring non-refundable deposits), predatory referral scams, or purely commercial advertisements without developer benefits.

# Evaluation guidance

Prioritize authenticity, actual cost savings, accessibility (ease of claiming), and duration of the free resources. Explicitly note prerequisites such as real-name identity verification, student ID verification, credit card requirement, or required task steps. Do not reward exaggerated clickbait headlines; focus on what developers can truly claim.
